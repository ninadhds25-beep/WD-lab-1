Tribute to the Voice of Emotion

Biography

Krishnakumar Kunnath, popularly known as KK, was one of India's most loved playback singers. He was known for his soulful voice and emotional songs. KK sang in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi and Bengali languages. His songs remain timeless and loved by fans.

Popular Songs

  • Khuda Jaane
  • Zara Sa
  • Tu Hi Meri Shab Hai

Achievements
