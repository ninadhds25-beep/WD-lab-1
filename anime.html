Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

A Popular Japanese Anime Series

About the Anime

Demon Slayer is an action-packed anime that follows the journey of Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is attacked by demons.

The story is written by Koyoharu Gotouge .

Main Characters

Tanjiro Kamado – The brave demon slayer.

Nezuko Kamado – Tanjiro's sister who becomes a demon.

Zenitsu Agatsuma – A powerful but fearful fighter.

Inosuke Hashibira – The wild swordsman.
